<h1>
  Snake Game
</h1>
<img src="Game.png" alt="text" width="100%" height="auto">
A classic Snake Game built with vanilla JavaScript, featuring smooth gameplay, catchy background music, and satisfying sound effects.
Challenge yourself and your friends — see who can score the highest!

🎮 Features
✅ Smooth & responsive snake movement
✅ Dynamic speed increase as your score grows
✅ High score tracking (saved in local storage)
✅ Fun sound effects and background music
✅ Clean, colorful user interface

🧠 Tech Stack
<li>HTML5</li>
<li>CSS3</li>
<li>JavaScript (Vanilla)</li>

🚀 How to Play
<li>Arrow Keys to move the snake</li>
<li>Eat food to grow and increase your score</li>
<li>Avoid walls and your own tail</li>
<li>Game speed increases every 5 points — can you keep up?</li>

🏆 Goal
Game speed increases every 5 points — can you keep up?
