---

### 🎮 Features  
✅ Smooth & responsive snake movement  
✅ Dynamic speed increase as your score grows  
✅ High score tracking (saved in local storage)  
✅ Fun sound effects and background music  
✅ Clean, colorful user interface  

---

### 🧠 Tech Stack  
<li>HTML5</li>
<li>CSS3</li>
<li>JavaScript (Vanilla)</li>

---

### 🚀 How to Play  
<li>Use <b>Arrow Keys</b> to move the snake</li>
<li>Eat food to grow and increase your score</li>
<li>Avoid walls and your own tail</li>
<li>Game speed increases every 5 points — can you keep up?</li>

---

### 🏆 Goal  
Eat as much food as possible and beat the high score!
